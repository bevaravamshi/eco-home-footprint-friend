📊 User Engagement Metrics

  • Daily Active Users (DAU): Number of unique users completing calculations daily
  • Completion Rate: Percentage of users who finish all 4 steps vs. those who start
  • Return Usage Rate: Percentage of users who calculate their footprint more than once
  • Step Drop-off Rate: At which step users most commonly abandon the calculator

📈 Content Performance

  • Category Interaction: Which carbon categories (Energy, Transport, Food, Waste) users spend most time on
  • Recommendation Click-through: Percentage of users who engage with the recommendation cards
  • Average Session Duration: Time spent on calculator
  • Page Views per Session: Engagement depth

🌍 Impact Measurement

  • Average Carbon Footprint: Mean CO₂ emissions calculated across all users
  • Improvement Tracking: For returning users, percentage reduction in carbon footprint over time
  • Awareness Score: Pre/post calculator survey on carbon awareness
  • Action Implementation: Users who report implementing recommendations

Short-term (1-3 months)

  • Achieve 70% completion rate for calculator
  • Reach 100 daily active users
  • Maintain <5% drop-off rate at any single step
  • Implement basic analytics tracking

Medium-term (3-6 months)

  • 500+ unique users monthly
  • 25% return user rate
  • Average 15% carbon footprint reduction for returning users
  • Launch user accounts for tracking progress

Long-term (6-12 months)

  • 2,000+ monthly active users
  • 40% of users implement at least 2 recommendations
  • Partner with 3+ environmental organizations for data validation
  • Mobile app or PWA version launch

✅ Highly Achievable

  • 70% completion rate - Your calculator has a clean, 4-step design that's user-friendly
  • <5% drop-off rate per step - The progress bar and clear navigation make this very doable
  • Analytics implementation - Can be done in 1-2 weeks with tools like Google Analytics

⚠️ Moderately Achievable

  • 100 daily users in 1-3 months - Depends on your marketing/distribution strategy
  • 25% return user rate - Environmental tools often have engaged audiences
  • 500+ monthly users in 3-6 months - Realistic with SEO and social sharing

❌ Challenging

  • 2,000+ monthly users in 6-12 months - Needs content marketing, partnerships, or viral growth
  • 40% of users implementing 2+ recommendations - This is quite high for behavioral change
  • Partnership with 3+ organizations - Depends on networking and outreach efforts

🚀 Recommended Starting Points

Conservative Initial Targets:

  • Month 1: 50 daily users, 60% completion rate
  • Month 3: 200 daily users, 70% completion rate
  • Month 6: 500 daily users, 20% return rate

The calculator itself is well-designed for user engagement, so UX-related metrics are very realistic. The challenge will be user acquisition and measuring real-world impact.
